AI Support-Ticket Triage Agent

A full web app that turns a raw IT support ticket into a structured, routable, SLA-tagged record with a drafted reply — then manages the whole ticket lifecycle. It encodes real service-desk judgment (e.g. anything resembling phishing auto-escalates to Security).

  • AI triage: priority, routing, sentiment, drafted reply
  • Accounts & login (PBKDF2 hashing, per-user data)
  • SQLite persistence with full activity history
  • Search, filters, replies, close/reopen, dashboard
  • Swappable LLM backend (Claude CLI or API)
  • Zero external dependencies — stdlib Python + vanilla JS
